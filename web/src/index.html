What this first press handles

PDF input, margin trimming, one- or two-column reading order, graphical word wrapping, fit-width, and fit-page output. Password-protected PDFs, OCR, selectable output text, tables, and complex diagrams are not yet reconstructed semantically.

Paprika runs on Rust and WebAssembly. Local-only · no upload
