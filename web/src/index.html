What Paprika preserves

Born-digital text stays selectable and reflows to your reader settings. Paprika detects columns, headings, lists, tables, links, and embedded images. Equations, vector diagrams, unusual layouts, and scanned pages can still need the experimental raster PDF fallback; OCR is not included yet.

Paprika runs on Rust and WebAssembly. Local-only · no upload
